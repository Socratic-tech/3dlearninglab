    WELCOME TO MAKESPACE

    From “what if” to “I made that.”

    1. Choose a challenge.

      Each project has a question to explore and rules that keep its parts connected.

    2. Make it yours.

      Move sliders or type sizes. Dimensions are in millimeters. Some projects let you view and download parts separately.

    3. Predict and test.

      Record your settings and observations in the Design notebook. Use evidence to improve your next design.

    4. Make something real.

      Download an STL for your slicer or another CAD tool. Save a Project ZIP to keep your editable work and notes.

    A 3D preview checks geometry, not real-world strength or printer fit. Check the model and orientation in your slicer before printing. Preview colors do not change STL printing colors.

    Keep creating with your STL.

    1. Download your STL.

      For a multi-part project, choose all parts or just one using Show above the preview.

    2. Open your next tool.

      In SimpCAD X, use Import. In Tinkercad, open a design and choose Import. In a slicer, open or drag in the STL. Use millimeters if asked.

    3. Keep your editable original.

      An STL keeps the shape. Your Project ZIP keeps the recipe, settings, files, and notebook so you can return and change the fit.
