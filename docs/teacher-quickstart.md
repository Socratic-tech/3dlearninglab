# 3D Design Academy — teacher quick start

Three steps, about 5 minutes. You need a school Google account. Your students' work is saved in **your own Google Sheet and Drive**.

**Just want a look first?** Open the website and click **Try it now**. No account, nothing to set up.

## Step 1. Make your copy and allow it
1. Open the website and click **Make my copy**, then **Make a copy** in Google Sheets.
2. In your new Sheet, click the menu **3D Design Academy → Set up & class links**. (No menu? Wait a few seconds or reload.)
3. Google asks for permission. You'll see **“Google hasn't verified this app.”** That's expected: it's *your* copy, not a
   third-party app. Click **Advanced → Go to 3D Design Academy (unsafe) → Allow**.
4. **Click the menu again.** The first click only asks for permission.

## Step 2. Turn on your app (one time, 2 minutes)
The panel prepares your Sheet by itself, then shows a picture of each screen:
**Open the script editor → Deploy → New deployment → ⚙ Web app → Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone → Deploy**
(click **Authorize access → Allow** if asked). The panel moves on by itself when it's done.
Google requires the teacher to make this click; nothing can do it for you.

## Step 3. Make a class and share the link
Type a class name, pick **18 weeks** or **9 weeks**, and click **Create class**.
Click **Copy student link** and post it in Google Classroom, or **Show QR code** for the board.

Click **Open my dashboard** to import rosters from Google Classroom, watch the **Right now** panel during
class, review work, and run the **print queue**. Bookmark the page that opens.

## Good to know
- **Teacher guides and slides:** every lesson has a teacher guide (Lessons tab → **Teacher guide**) with background
  reading, the goal and success criteria, a minute-by-minute plan, questions students ask, and an exit ticket.
  **Download slides** makes a deck for that lesson; **Download all (.zip)** on the Lessons tab makes one for every
  lesson in your course. To use them in Google Slides, upload the file to Drive and choose **Open with → Google
  Slides** (or, in a deck you already have, **File → Import slides**). Your teacher moves are in the speaker notes.
- **Who can sign in:** your email domain is filled in for you. If students use a different domain
  (like `students.district.org`), add it under **Settings** in the panel.
- **Updates:** the Sheet contains a small loader plus its manifest. The app and lessons come from the website, check for updates automatically, and keep a cached backup for outages. You do not have to replace script files after setup.
- **“Anyone” access** only lets the website reach your Sheet. Every student still signs in with Google, and only
  students on your roster get in. If your district blocks “Anyone” web apps or unverified apps, ask your Google admin to
  allow this one script.
