## Product standard: UDL Guidelines 3.0

Treat CAST's Universal Design for Learning Guidelines 3.0 as a standing requirement for every student-facing change.

- Design multiple means of engagement, representation, and action and expression without lowering the learning goal.
- Use learner-centered, plain language; explain necessary technical vocabulary where students encounter it.
- Preserve learner choice and agency, including multiple ways to understand directions and communicate evidence.
- Make goals, progress, requirements, feedback, and next actions explicit and easy to monitor.
- Support keyboard, touch, voice, read-aloud, reduced-vision, and varied screen-size access.
- Test changes from the perspective of learner variability, belonging, dignity, and independence—not only technical correctness.
